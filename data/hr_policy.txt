TECHCORP HR POLICY MANUAL
Version 2.0 | Effective: January 2025

===========================================
SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY
===========================================
1.1 Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave must be applied at least 3 days in advance through the HR portal.
Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days to the next year.
Leave encashment is permitted only at the time of resignation or retirement.

1.2 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
Medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.
Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed.

1.3 Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.
Maternity leave can be availed from 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.
Applicable from the first day of employment with no minimum tenure required.

1.4 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 5 days of paid paternity leave.
Must be taken within 30 days of the child's birth.

1.5 Emergency Leave
Up to 3 days of emergency leave can be granted for immediate family emergencies.
Approval from the department head is mandatory.

===========================================
SECTION 2: WORK HOURS & REMOTE WORK
===========================================
2.1 Standard Work Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
Employees are entitled to a 1-hour lunch break.
Total working hours per week: 40 hours.

2.2 Flexible Work Arrangement
Employees may apply for flexible hours with manager approval.
Core hours (mandatory presence): 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Flexible arrangement must be reviewed every 3 months.

2.3 Remote Work Policy
Employees are eligible for up to 2 days of remote work per week.
Remote work requires a stable internet connection and a dedicated workspace.
Employees must be reachable during core hours while working remotely.
Remote work is not applicable during the probation period (first 6 months).

2.4 Overtime
Overtime work must be pre-approved by the department manager.
Overtime compensation: 1.5x the hourly rate for weekdays.
Weekend overtime compensation: 2x the hourly rate.

===========================================
SECTION 3: CODE OF CONDUCT
===========================================
3.1 Professional Behaviour
Employees must maintain a respectful and inclusive work environment.
Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind is strictly prohibited.
Violations will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

3.2 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose company information to external parties.
Confidentiality obligations continue for 2 years after employment ends.
Breach of confidentiality may result in legal action.

3.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any outside employment or business interests.
Working for a direct competitor during employment is strictly prohibited.
Gifts above INR 1000 in value from vendors must be reported to HR.

3.4 Social Media Policy
Employees must not post confidential company information on social media.
Personal opinions must be clearly distinguished from company positions.
Violations may result in disciplinary action.

===========================================
SECTION 4: COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
===========================================
4.1 Salary Structure
Salaries are reviewed annually every April.
Performance appraisals determine increment percentages.
Increment range: 5% to 20% based on performance rating.

4.2 Health Insurance
All employees receive health insurance coverage of INR 5,00,000 per year.
Coverage extends to spouse and up to 2 dependent children.
Pre-existing conditions are covered after a 1-year waiting period.

4.3 Provident Fund
Company contributes 12% of basic salary to the Employee Provident Fund.
Employee contribution is also 12% of basic salary.
PF withdrawal is permitted after resignation or retirement.

4.4 Performance Bonus
Annual performance bonus ranges from 5% to 15% of CTC.
Bonus is disbursed in the month of May each year.
Employees must complete at least 6 months of service to be eligible.

4.5 Travel Allowance
Employees travelling for official purposes are reimbursed as per company grade.
Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of travel.
Air travel requires prior approval from department head and HR.
